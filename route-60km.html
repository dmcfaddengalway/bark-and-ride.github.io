60km Distance
36m Elevation Gain
2–3h Est. Duration

View Route on Strava
About This Route

The perfect introduction to Bark & Ride. This scenic 60km loop takes in the gentle rolling terrain south of the Burren, quiet country roads, picturesque villages, and lush green farmland. Ideal for first-time sportive riders or anyone looking for a fun, achievable challenge.

Route Highlights
  • Dunguaire Castle A 16th-century tower house on the shores of Kinvara Bay — one of Ireland's most photographed castles.
  • Dromore Wood Nature Reserve A beautiful oak woodland and lake system — a stunning early stretch to ease you into the ride.
  • Corofin Village One of Clare's most beloved heritage villages, rich in traditional music culture.
  • Inagh Valley Views Panoramic views across the valley floor before looping back to Galway.
Elevation

Mostly flat to gently rolling terrain. No major climbs — total elevation gain of just 36m. Very suitable for riders of all fitness levels.

Rider Tips
  • ✓Great for first-time sportive riders — pace is relaxed and support is excellent.
  • ✓Start hydrated. Clare's exposed roads can be deceptively windy.
  • ✓Pack a light rain jacket — weather changes quickly in the Burren.
  • ✓Don't race — soak in the scenery and the atmosphere.
