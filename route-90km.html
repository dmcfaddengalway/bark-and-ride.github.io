90kmKilmacduagh Round Tower

Taking in the iconic Kilmacduagh Round Tower and the dramatic karst landscape that defines the Burren, this 90km route is the heart of Bark & Ride.

90km Distance
36m Elevation Gain
3–4.5h Est. Duration
3 Food Stops
View Route on Strava
About This Route

The heart of Bark & Ride. This 90km route tackles meaningful climbs, sweeps through exposed limestone plateaus, and descends to the Atlantic coast before climbing back inland. You'll pass one of Ireland's finest round towers and round the wild headland at Black Head. A proper test with proper rewards.

Route Highlights
  • Kilmacduagh Round Tower One of Ireland's finest, leaning slightly and standing over 30 metres tall — a breathtaking landmark as you pass through quiet countryside south of Galway.
  • Corkscrew Hill The signature climb of this route — a winding ascent through hazel woodland with increasingly dramatic views over Galway Bay.
  • Ballyvaughan & Black Head The route hugs the coast through Ballyvaughan before rounding the wild, windswept headland at Black Head.
  • Aillwee Approach A testing climb through the heart of the Burren's lunar landscape, with the Aillwee Cave system hidden in the hillside above.
Elevation

Undulating with two significant climbs: Corkscrew Hill (Category 3, 2.5km at 7%) and the Aillwee approach (3km at 5%). Several shorter sharp rises along the coastal section. Total elevation gain 36m.

Rider Tips
  • ✓Pace yourself on Corkscrew Hill — it comes around the 45km mark and you'll need energy for the return leg.
  • ✓The coastal stretch from Ballyvaughan to Black Head can be very exposed to Atlantic winds. Tuck in behind other riders if you can.
  • ✓Make full use of the three food stops — proper fuelling is the difference between enjoying the last 20km and surviving it.
  • ✓Carry a spare tube and know how to change it. The limestone roads can be rough on tyres.
